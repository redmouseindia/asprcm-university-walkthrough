What is needed to finish the pending items

Everything that could be built at no cost is built. What is left needs a paid service, an approval, or a setting from IT. Each item below lists what it would complete, and what it needs.

1. An AI service for text

Paid · usage based

This one service completes most of the pending lines.

It completes

  • AI writing the text of posters, flyers, teasers and knowledge bites, and suggesting the layout (area 3)
  • AI writing video scripts, summarising long content, and picking the key points for knowledge-bite videos (area 4)
  • AI questions written from Word, PDF and PowerPoint files, and question banks filled automatically (area 5)

It needs

  • A choice of provider, for example Anthropic Claude or Azure OpenAI
  • A company account with billing, and a monthly usage budget
  • An API key, kept on the server only
  • Approval to send training content to that provider. The content sent must not contain patient data, and compliance may require a data agreement with the provider

Who: management (budget and approval), IT (account and key)

2. An AI image service

Paid · per picture

Only needed if pictures should be drawn by AI.

It completes

  • AI-drawn graphics and pictures for posters and flyers (area 3: Graphics, Visual Content)

It needs

  • An image service, often from the same provider as item 1
  • A budget per picture, and the same approval as item 1

Who: management, IT

Without it, admins add their own pictures, as they can today.

3. A paid voice service

Paid · per minute of audio

Only needed if the free voices are not good enough.

It completes

  • Studio-quality professional voice-over (area 4)
  • A Tamil voice (area 4: Accent)

It needs

  • A voice provider, for example Azure Speech, Google Cloud Text-to-Speech or ElevenLabs
  • An account, an API key and a budget per minute of audio

Who: management, IT

4. Settings from IT

No cost

Small jobs on the server, no budget needed.

It completes

  • Indian English narration voices (area 4: Accent)
  • Emails actually delivered: notifications, reminders, messages and scheduled reports (areas 3 and 8)

It needs

  • "English (India)" speech voices added in Windows Settings on the server; they then appear in the voice list by themselves
  • The company's Microsoft 365 or SMTP mail settings and a sender mailbox, entered in Admin › Email

Who: IT

Decisions needed

  1. Approve a budget: about US$1,500 a year (≈ ₹1.32 lakh + GST) for the recommended option, with monthly spending limits set at each provider.
  2. Choose the AI provider (recommended: Anthropic Claude), and say whether AI pictures are wanted (item 2).
  3. Approve sending training content to that provider (with a check that it contains no patient data).
  4. Decide whether the free voices are enough, or a paid voice service is wanted for studio quality and Tamil (item 3).
  5. Ask IT for the mail settings and the Indian English voices (item 4).

Once a provider and key are in place, the AI features plug into screens that already exist: the poster editor, the video scene editor, and the question review and approval steps. AI only makes drafts; a person reviews and approves them before anything is published.
